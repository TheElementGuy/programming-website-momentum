Design Subteam

- At the very start of the build season when the game content and game manual are revealed, we do the following:

  • Discuss with fellow members of design about how to design mechanisms around constraints
  • Compare to previous years for inspiration
  • Brainstorm ideas for how our robot might look

- Use design matrixes to determine our main strategic objective within matches

  • What our robot focuses on
  • What is valuable for us to have in real matches

- Come together to brainstorm specific mechanisms within the robot

  • What mechanism scores pieces, defense, etc.

- Prototype different mechanisms and see how effective they are with the game piece(s)

- Create low and high-detail CADs in Onshape

- Work through a multistage peer and mentor review process

- Coordinate designs between fellow designers and manufacturers

- Create a Bill of Materials (BOM) to tell us what we would need to buy

- Guide manufacturers in the building process

  • Show members of the manufacturing team how to navigate the cad
  • Explain complex systems
  • Assist with confusion/questions

- Review the design(s) and see what does and does not work well
