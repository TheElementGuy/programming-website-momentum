Momentum Robotics: Business

Are you interested in helping your community, learning how to write grants for large companies, or product design? Then the business subteam is the subteam for you!

You will be able to help support our team’s funding by learning how to write grants and host fundraisers!

You will also be able to design merchandise, create posts, and other promotional material!

During the season, you will help the business team apply for FRC awards, posts, promotional materials, merch. We spend a lot of the season working on awards. Most notably, the Impact award. It is the most prestigious award in FRC, and we need a group of people to dedicate their time to this project during the season. Also during the season we create social media posts. During the off season, we will apply for grants, and throughout the year and try to host more fundraisers!

What we do:

  • Media
  • Outreach
  • Social Media Posts
  • Branding
  • Logistics
  • Awards
  • Grants
  • Fundraising
  • Finance Tracking
