Momentum Robotics:
Manufacturing

Do you like building and manufacturing? Do you want to build robots? Well now's your chance!

We are the manufacturing subteam. We are the ones who actually make the robot what it is, building the CAD, wiring the robot, doing robot assembly, and the basis of the robot works.

In manufacturing, we do all things relating to assembly and electrical. We do whatever it takes to build a functioning robot and repair that robot if necessary.

There’s no prior experience needed! Every tool, piece of equipment, and materials are all provided/bought, so there’s no worry about bringing your own. We also teach how to use everything as well. One big core concept of manufacturing is safety. Safety is good! Like the tools, safety procedures are taught and practiced very often, so following the rules at all times is very important.

What we do:

  • Assembly of robot
  • Take your time
  • Work based off the CAD
  • Cutting and Drilling
  • Wiring - programmers can't work without this
  • Learn to work with various tools
  • Practice safety
